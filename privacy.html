AnyApps.in — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps published under AnyApps.in. Each app's data is kept completely isolated — AnyApps.in staff cannot view, access, or process your personal records without authorization. Your data is never sold or shared with any third party.

What We Collect

• Your name, email, and phone number (for account/organization signup)
• The records you create within each app (entries, bookings, transactions, attendance, etc. — specific to that app's purpose)
• Your Telegram Chat ID, only if you choose to connect Telegram — used solely to deliver alerts and notifications
• Your password, stored securely (we cannot read it)

We do NOT collect your location, contacts, photos, or any other device information beyond what each app needs to function.

Where Your Data Lives

By default, all data is encrypted and stored on servers located within India. For European client projects, EU-region (Frankfurt) hosting can be set up on request — see our Security page for details, or contact us to discuss what's right for your project.

Access Control

Each organization or account's data is kept fully separate from every other user on our platform. Within an app, an Admin may see all records under their organization; a Staff Member or regular user can only see records assigned to or created by them. New organizations may require approval before their account becomes active, as an added layer of control.

Telegram Notifications

If you choose to connect Telegram on any app, we use your Telegram Chat ID only to send you relevant alerts and notices. You can disconnect at any time by contacting us, and we will stop sending messages to that chat.

Data Deletion

You may request complete deletion of your account or organization's data at any time by contacting us at vkvcoder.support@gmail.com. We will delete all your records within 7 working days.
